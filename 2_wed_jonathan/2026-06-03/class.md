Це вже хороший набір на рівень B2+/C1. Тут багато типових пасток: інверсія, герундій, despite, relative clauses, conditionals, countable/uncountable nouns тощо.

1.

❌ Rarely the local authorities encountered…

✔ Rarely did the local authorities encounter such widespread public resistance during last year’s urban redevelopment scheme.

👉 Після негативних прислівників (Rarely, Seldom, Never, Little, Hardly) потрібна інверсія:

* Rarely did they encounter…
* Never have I seen…

2.

❌ considering to postpone

✔ The board of directors is seriously considering postponing the annual shareholder meeting until next quarter.

👉 consider + gerund (-ing)

Правильно:

* consider doing
* avoid doing
* suggest doing

Неправильно:

* consider to do

3.

❌ Despite the technical team worked…

✔ Despite the technical team working around the clock, they were unable to restore the server before morning.

✔ Although the technical team worked around the clock, they were unable to restore the server before morning.

👉

despite + noun / gerund

although + clause

Порівняй:

* Despite working hard…
* Although they worked hard…

4.

❌ what has significantly boosted employee morale

✔ The company has decided to offer remote work permanently, which has significantly boosted employee morale.

👉 Після коми для додаткової інформації використовуємо:

which

НЕ

what

5.

❌ змішані часові рамки

✔ If the architect had drafted the blueprint correctly last month, the construction crew would not be facing these structural issues now.

або

✔ If the architect had drafted the blueprint correctly last month, the construction crew would not have faced these structural issues last week.

👉 Умовне речення повинно бути логічним за часом.

Було:

* cause → last month
* result → last week

Нелогічно.

6.

❌ fewer evidence

✔ The leaked memo contained less evidence of corporate corruption than the investigative journalists had anticipated.

👉

evidence = uncountable

Тому:

* less evidence ✔
* fewer pieces of evidence ✔
* fewer evidence ❌

7.

❌ was occurred

✔ The unexpected breakthrough in quantum computing occurred while the researchers were experimenting with new superconductors.

👉

occur не може бути passive.

Так само:

* happen
* occur
* exist
* belong

8.

❌ has completely almost rewritten

✔ The software engineering team has almost completely rewritten the codebase to fix the security vulnerability.

або

✔ The software engineering team has completely rewritten the codebase…

👉 Порядок прислівників.

Зазвичай:

almost completely

НЕ

completely almost

9.

❌ No sooner … when

✔ No sooner had the new policy been announced than the employees began to voice their concerns.

👉 Фіксована конструкція:

No sooner … than

НЕ

No sooner … when

10.

❌ Founding in 1923

✔ Founded in 1923, the charitable organization has spent over a century providing aid to underprivileged communities.

👉 Організація не “founding”.

Її founded (заснували).

⸻

11

❌ Even if he loves his wife very much…

✔ Even though he loves his wife very much, he has decided they should spend more time apart.

👉

Even if = якщо раптом

Even though = хоча

Тут потрібен контраст.

⸻

12

❌ to not give up

✔ The mentor advised her student not to give up on the research project despite the initial setbacks.

👉 Стандартний порядок:

advise someone not to do something

⸻

Швидка шпаргалка
