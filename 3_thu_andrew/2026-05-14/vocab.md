## Formal Letter — Set Phrases

### Opening paragraph
- [ ] I am writing to complain about / make a complaint about …
- [ ] I am writing to you regarding / in connection with …
- [ ] I feel I must complain about …
- [ ] I regret that I am obliged to complain about …
- [ ] Unfortunately, it was nothing like what I expected.

### Middle paragraphs
- [ ] I must point out that …
- [ ] To my amazement / surprise, …
- [ ] Your advertisement / brochure was misleading in this respect.
- [ ] As if that was not bad enough, …
- [ ] You led me to believe that …
- [ ] You failed to mention that …
- [ ] I was shocked / surprised / disappointed …

### Closing paragraph
- [ ] Considering all the above, I believe I am entitled to a partial/full refund.
- [ ] I demand a full refund / immediate action.
- [ ] I would appreciate it if we could sort this matter out as soon as possible.
- [ ] I feel confident that this matter will receive your prompt attention.
- [ ] I look forward to hearing from you.
- [ ] Thanking you in advance.

### Salutation rules
- [ ] Dear Sir / Madam → **Yours faithfully**
- [ ] Dear Mr/Mrs/Ms [Last Name] → **Yours sincerely**
- [ ] To Whom It May Concern → **Yours faithfully**

## Linking Words for Complaint Letters

| Function | Words/Phrases |
|----------|---------------|
| Listing points | firstly, to begin with, for a start, secondly, finally |
| Adding information | furthermore, moreover, in addition, besides this, what is more |
| Emphasising | in fact, apparently, obviously, not only … but also |
| Contrast | however, although, despite the fact that, contrary to, whereas |
| Result/consequence | therefore, as a result, for this reason, as a consequence |
| Summarising | all in all, on the whole |
