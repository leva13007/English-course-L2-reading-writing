Complaint About Kim’s Wonder Diet Service

Dear Sir or Madam,

I am writing to complain about the service I recently received from Kim’s Wonder Diet programme. Unfortunately, the experience was completely different from what was advertised in your brochure.

To begin with, your advertisement promised that customers would “lose weight in three weeks”. However, after following the programme carefully, I actually gained two kilos. Furthermore, although you claimed that the meals would be “healthy and delicious”, the food was completely tasteless and extremely disappointing.

I must also point out that your advertisement stated customers would pay only half price for meals if they joined immediately. To my surprise, I was charged the full amount without any explanation. In addition, the meals were often delivered late and sometimes arrived cold, which made them unpleasant to eat.

As if that was not bad enough, it was almost impossible to contact your company. Whenever I called the number provided, I reached only an answering machine and received no response to my messages.

Considering all the above, I believe I am entitled to a full refund. I would appreciate it if you could deal with this matter immediately. I look forward to hearing from you.

Yours faithfully,
