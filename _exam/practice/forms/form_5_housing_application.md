**1. Why are you applying for council housing at this time, and why is your current situation unsuitable?**

I am applying for council housing at this time because my family has outgrown our current rented flat and we are now living in overcrowded conditions. My wife, our two children and I currently share a two-bedroom flat, which means our children do not each have their own space to sleep or study. The property is also becoming increasingly expensive, and our private landlord has indicated that the rent will rise again next year.

**2. Describe your ideal property and the area of Cardiff where you would prefer to live. Explain your reasons.**

My ideal property would be a three-bedroom house with a small garden, where our children would have room to play safely. I would prefer to live in the Roath or Cathays area of Cardiff, as it is close to my children's current school and within walking distance of local shops and healthcare services. Staying in a familiar area would also help to minimise disruption to our children's education.

**3. Is there any additional information you feel the council should consider when reviewing your application?**

I would like the council to be aware that both of our children currently share a single bedroom, which is having a noticeable impact on their sleep and concentration at school. We have lived in Cardiff for several years and have strong ties to the local community. I would be grateful if our application could be considered as a priority given these circumstances.

- The household table, "people living in property" number, and priority-needs tick box (overcrowding) all match the explanation given in Section 3

- Q2 explains the *reasons* for the preferred area, not just the area itself, as the question asks
- Formal, respectful tone throughout — appropriate for a request to a public authority
- No contractions anywhere in the written responses
