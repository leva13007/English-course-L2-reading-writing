- *I am applying for council housing because my current accommodation is no longer suitable due to...*
- *I have been living in my current property for [LENGTH OF TIME], however...*
- *I would prefer to live in [AREA] as it is close to [SCHOOL / WORK / TRANSPORT]...*
- *I would like the council to be aware that [CIRCUMSTANCE], which significantly affects...*
- *I believe that suitable social housing would greatly improve the quality of life for myself and my family.*
