**1. Describe your overall experience during your stay, including the room and the staff service.**

Overall, my three-night stay at The Cardiff Bay Hotel was very enjoyable. My room was clean, comfortable and well-equipped, with a pleasant view over the bay. The staff at reception were consistently friendly and helpful, and they dealt with my queries quickly and efficiently throughout my stay.

**2. What did you like most about your stay, and was there anything that could be improved?**

What I liked most about my stay was the quality of the breakfast in the hotel restaurant, which offered a wide variety of fresh, well-prepared food. I also appreciated being able to use the gym free of charge during my visit. One aspect that could be improved, however, is the Wi-Fi connection, which was unreliable in some areas of the hotel.

**3. Would you recommend this hotel to others? Explain your reasons.**

I would definitely recommend this hotel to friends and family because of its excellent staff, comfortable rooms and convenient location near the bay. Although the Wi-Fi connection could be improved, this did not significantly affect my overall experience. I would be happy to stay at this hotel again in the future.
