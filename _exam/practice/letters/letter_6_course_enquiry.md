## Model Letter *(~290 words — formal register, enquiry to education centre)*

---

31 Newport Road
Cardiff
CF24 0AB

30 June 2026

The Admissions Team
Cardiff Adult Education Centre
25 Charles Street
Cardiff
CF10 2GA

Dear Sir or Madam,

I am writing to enquire about the adult education courses available at Cardiff Adult Education Centre, having seen your recent advertisement in Cardiff Central Library. I am very interested in enrolling on a course and would be grateful if you could provide further information to help me make an informed decision.

I am particularly interested in the IT and business courses listed in your advertisement. Could you please advise on the level and content of these programmes? Specifically, I would like to know whether they are available at different levels — for example, Level 2 and Level 3 — and whether any prior qualifications or experience are required for entry. I would also be grateful to know whether the courses are assessed through examinations, coursework, or a combination of both.

In addition, I have some practical questions regarding the schedule and cost of the courses. Are classes available in the evenings or at weekends, as I am currently in full-time employment during the day? I would also like to know what the course fees are and whether there are any grants, bursaries, or government funding options available for adult learners, as I understand that some financial support may be available through the Welsh Government for eligible students.

I would be very grateful if you could send me a prospectus or course guide, together with details of how to apply. I would also be happy to attend an open day or information session if one is available.

I look forward to hearing from you at your earliest convenience.

Yours faithfully,

Oleh Levchenko

---

**What makes this letter good:**
- *Dear Sir or Madam* → *Yours faithfully* (unknown recipient)
- Questions are grouped logically: content first, then practical details
- "Could you please..." and "I would be grateful if..." — polite, not demanding
- Ends with a concrete next step (prospectus + open day)
- No emotional language — purely informational and polite
