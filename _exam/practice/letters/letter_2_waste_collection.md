## Model Letter *(~260 words — formal register, complaint to council)*

---

5 Splott Road
Cardiff
CF24 2BZ

30 June 2026

The Waste Services Team
Cardiff Council
City Hall
Cardiff
CF10 3ND

Dear Sir or Madam,

I am writing to express my dissatisfaction with the waste and recycling collection service currently provided in the Splott area of Cardiff. Over the past three months, I have experienced repeated problems which I believe require your urgent attention.

The most significant issue is the frequency of missed collections. On at least four occasions in recent months, both my general waste bin and my recycling bin were not collected on the scheduled date, and in two of those instances they were not emptied for over two weeks. I received no notification or apology on any of these occasions, which I found unacceptable.

In addition, it appears that recycling bins have not been provided consistently across the area. Several of my neighbours have informed me that they have never received a recycling bin, despite requesting one on multiple occasions. This makes it impossible for all households in the area to participate in the council's own recycling scheme.

I would therefore urge the council to investigate the reason for the missed collections and implement a more reliable schedule. Furthermore, I would strongly recommend that the council conducts an audit of all households in the area to ensure that every property has been supplied with the correct recycling containers.

I hope that this matter will be addressed promptly. I look forward to receiving a response within fourteen days.

Yours faithfully,

Oleh Levchenko

---

**What makes this letter good:**
- *Dear Sir or Madam* → *Yours faithfully* (recipient unknown)
- Specific numbers ("four occasions", "two weeks") — not vague
- Logical progression: problem → detail → consequence → request
- No emotional language — professional tone throughout
