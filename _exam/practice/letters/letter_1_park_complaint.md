## Model Letter *(~285 words — formal register, complaint to named MP)*

---

14 Roath Court Road
Cardiff
CF24 3AA

30 June 2026

Ms Sarah Murphy, MP
Cardiff East Constituency Office
23 Albany Road
Cardiff
CF24 3RP

Dear Ms Murphy,

I am writing to express my serious concern about the deteriorating condition of Roath Park in Cardiff. As a local resident and regular visitor, I have observed a significant decline in its maintenance over recent months, which is having a damaging effect on the local community.

Firstly, the litter problem in the park has become very difficult to manage. Rubbish is regularly left scattered across the grass and pathways, particularly near the café and children's play area. This is made considerably worse by the fact that there are very few litter bins available, and those that do exist are frequently left overflowing for several days at a time. As a result, the park has become an unpleasant and unhygienic environment for families and residents.

Furthermore, a number of items of playground equipment are currently broken or in a dangerous state of disrepair. Several swings have missing or damaged seats, and the main climbing frame has been unsafe for children to use for over two months. This is a particular concern for families with young children, who depend on the park as a safe outdoor space.

I would strongly urge you to raise these issues with the relevant authorities and request that the council introduces a regular maintenance schedule for the park. In addition, I would suggest that the number of litter bins is increased and that they are emptied on a daily basis.

I hope that you will treat this matter with urgency. I would be happy to provide further details or to meet at your convenience.

Yours sincerely,

Oleh Levchenko

---

**What makes this letter good:**
- Address block correct (sender top-right, recipient left)
- Named recipient → *Yours sincerely* (not faithfully)
- Each paragraph = one clear point with specific detail
- No emotional language ("appalled", "disgusted") — facts only
- Ends with a polite offer, not a demand
