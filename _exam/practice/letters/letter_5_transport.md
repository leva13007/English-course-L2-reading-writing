## Model Letter *(~280 words — formal register, suggestion to council)*

---

7 Cowbridge Road East
Cardiff
CF11 9AB

30 June 2026

The Transport Planning Team
Cardiff Council
City Hall
Cardiff
CF10 3ND

Dear Sir or Madam,

I am writing to suggest several improvements to the cycling and public transport infrastructure in Cardiff, which I believe would significantly benefit residents and contribute to the council's own environmental targets.

As a daily cyclist, I am acutely aware of the dangers posed by the current road layout on several key routes. On Cowbridge Road East and City Road, for example, there are no dedicated cycling lanes, which means that cyclists must share road space with fast-moving traffic. This situation discourages many people from choosing to cycle and presents a genuine safety risk to those who do. I would strongly recommend that the council invests in a network of protected cycle lanes on these routes, similar to those already in place in Swansea and Newport.

In addition, the local bus service in my area is frequently unreliable. Buses on routes 17 and 62 are regularly delayed by fifteen minutes or more, and there are no real-time information displays at most bus stops. Furthermore, during peak hours the buses are often too crowded for all waiting passengers to board. I would suggest that the council introduces bus priority lanes on the busiest routes and works with Cardiff Bus to install digital real-time displays at all major stops.

These improvements would encourage more residents to use sustainable forms of transport, reduce traffic congestion in the city centre, and help Cardiff meet its carbon reduction targets for 2030. I hope that the council will give these proposals serious consideration.

Yours faithfully,

Oleh Levchenko

---

**What makes this letter good:**
- *Dear Sir or Madam* → *Yours faithfully* (unnamed team/department)
- Each suggestion paired immediately with a problem (not just a wish list)
- Specific route numbers make it concrete and credible
- Closing paragraph explains the wider benefit — not just "I want this"
