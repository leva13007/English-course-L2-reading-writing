## Model Letter *(~275 words — formal register, request to headteacher)*

---

42 Llanishen Street
Cardiff
CF14 3QA

30 June 2026

Mr David Morgan
Headteacher
Llanishen Primary School
Llanishen
Cardiff
CF14 5EB

Dear Mr Morgan,

I am writing on behalf of a number of parents at Llanishen Primary School to express our concern regarding the recent cancellation of after-school clubs. We fully understand that the school is facing financial pressures; however, we believe that the impact of this decision on the children and families at the school deserves further consideration.

After-school clubs play an important role in the development and wellbeing of pupils. They provide a safe and structured environment for children whose parents work full-time, and offer valuable opportunities to develop skills in sport, music, art, and other areas. Since the clubs were cancelled in March, several parents have informed me that they are experiencing significant difficulty in arranging adequate childcare for the period between the end of the school day and the end of the working day.

We are aware that funding is the main difficulty, and we would like to suggest some possible solutions. A number of local businesses have expressed an interest in sponsoring school activities, and there are also grants available through the Welsh Government specifically for extracurricular programmes. We would also be prepared to support a modest voluntary contribution from parents who are in a position to contribute.

We would be very grateful if you could arrange a meeting with a small group of parents at your earliest convenience to discuss these options. We are committed to working with the school to find a practical and fair solution.

Yours sincerely,

Oleh Levchenko
*(on behalf of Llanishen Primary School Parent Group)*

---

**What makes this letter good:**
- Written *on behalf of* a group — acknowledged clearly at the start and end
- Acknowledges the school's difficulty before making the request (shows understanding)
- Suggestions are specific, not vague ("Welsh Government grants", "local business sponsorship")
- Ends with an offer to collaborate — not a threat or demand
