# Exam Tasks — ESOL L2 Writing

Writing exam at Cardiff and Vale College · 13 July 2026

---

## Task 1 — Form with complex features (AC 3.1)
**Time:** 30 min

**Section 1 — Personal Details**
Fill in blanks with short factual answers.
- **Capitals / lower case must be correct** (e.g. "Oleh Levchenko", not "oleh levchenko")
- **Address order:** building number → street name → town/city → postcode
- All fields must be complete

**Section 2 — About You**
Multiple choice questions. **Follow each instruction exactly** — tick / circle / delete / underline as stated per question. Read carefully: some say "tick one box", others say "delete as appropriate". No full sentences needed here.

**Section 3 — Written Responses**
Open questions requiring written answers.
- **Must write at least 5 correct sentences in total** across all questions in this section
- Answers must be in full, grammatically correct sentences
- Appropriate formal register
- Accurate spelling and punctuation

Typical question themes:
- Why are you interested in this / what do you hope to gain?
- What skills or qualities do you have?
- What are your future career goals?
- Describe any relevant experience.

## Task 2 — Formal Writing (AC 1.1 + 2.1–2.6)
**Time:** 50 min · **Words:** ≥ 220 · **Pass: 5 out of 7 criteria**

One task from the following themes. You will not choose — the exam sets the theme.

| Theme | Typical prompt |
|-------|---------------|
| Formal letter / complaint to authority | Write to your MP / council about a local issue |
| Complaint letter (service/product) | Write to a company about a poor experience |
| Review | Write a review of a film / book / restaurant / album |
| Personal statement / application | Write a personal statement for a course or job |

### Assessment Criteria (7 total — pass 5)

| AC | Criterion | What the examiner looks for |
|----|-----------|----------------------------|
| 1.1 | **Plan** | Original ideas for all points · clear order and structure |
| 2.1 | **Purpose** | Correct format (letter/email/article) · consistent style · covers ALL points in the task |
| 2.2 | **Language** | Correct register (formal/neutral/informal as required) · **≥ 7 sentences with correct tenses** (mix of past/present/future) · upper-intermediate vocabulary |
| 2.3 | **Structure** | Correct paragraphs · easy to follow · **L2 discourse markers** used (Furthermore, However, In addition, Therefore...) |
| 2.4 | **Grammar** | Accurate grammar · **≥ 3 correct complex sentences** (relative/conditional clauses) · pronouns correct |
| 2.5 | **Punctuation** | **≥ 7 sentences with correct punctuation** · variety of marks (commas, colons, semicolons, apostrophes, etc.) |
| 2.6 | **Spelling** | Majority of common words, technical words, and key topic words spelled correctly |

**Important:** You must also pass each criterion at least once across the whole paper.

## Task 3 — Extended Writing
**Time:** 50 min · **Words:** ≥ 200

One task from the following themes:

| Theme | Structure |
|-------|-----------|
| **Essay** | Intro + 2 body paragraphs (positive + negative) + conclusion. 10 sentences minimum, ~20+ words each. |
| **Article** | Heading + engaging hook intro + 2–3 paragraphs + conclusion. Can use rhetorical questions. Semi-formal. |
| **Advice** | Opening + 3–4 advice points with reasons + polite closing. Formal or semi-formal. |

**Essay paragraph formula (4 sentences each):**
1. Topic sentence (main point)
2. Supporting example
3. Counterargument / contrast
4. Conclusion / link sentence

**Key marking criteria:**
- Complex sentence structures (subordinating conjunctions, conditionals, comparatives)
- Variety of linking words
- Consistent register
- Clear argument / progression of ideas
