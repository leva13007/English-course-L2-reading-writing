# Essay
50 min

Write an essay on one of the following topics. Use the correct register and formal style. Include an introduction, body paragraphs, and a conclusion. Aim for at least 200 words.

Introduction: Introduce the topic and your position. State your thesis (main argument). It could be a single sentence or a short paragraph. Make sure to clearly state your opinion.
Body paragraphs: Each paragraph should focus on a single point that supports your thesis. Start with a topic sentence, provide evidence or examples, and explain how it supports your argument. Use linking words to connect your ideas and ensure a logical flow. And use the conclusion sentence to summarise the paragraph and link it to the next one. Aim for at least 2 body paragraphs.
Conclusion: Summarise your main points and restate your thesis in a new way. End with a strong closing statement that reinforces your argument.

Mechanical way to do writing - in the body are two paragraphs, each with 4 sentences!
The structure of each paragraph is as follows:

Main topic or Topic sentence - 1 sentence
Supporting sentence - example - 1 sentence
Counterargument - contrast - 1 sentence
Conclusion - balance - 1 sentence

Introduction: 1 paragraph / 1 sentence
Body: 2 paragraphs / 8 sentences
Conclusion: 1 paragraph / 1 sentence

10 sentences in total, it means the sentence should have at least 20 words each. The total word count should be at least 200 words.

## Steps to write an essay

1. Read the essay prompt carefully and make sure you understand the topic and what is being asked of you.
2. Brainstorm ideas and create an outline to organise your thoughts(Write the plan!!!). Decide on your thesis statement and the main points you want to make in your essay.
3. Let's make first body paragraph as positive argument and second body paragraph as negative argument. Make sure to include evidence and examples to support your points.
4. In this way the conclusion will be a balance of both arguments and will be more persuasive.
5. Write the list of linking words and phrases you will use to connect your ideas and ensure a logical flow.
6. Write the introduction, body paragraphs, and conclusion. Use the outline as a guide and make sure to stay on topic and support your thesis with evidence and examples.
7. Use a formal style and avoid using contractions, slang, or informal language. Make sure to proofread your essay for grammar, spelling, and punctuation errors. Check that your essay is well-organised and that your ideas are clearly expressed. Make sure to use a variety of sentence structures and vocabulary to demonstrate your language proficiency.

List of linking words and phrases to use in your essay:
* first paragraph(positive): 
  Firstly, In the first place, To begin with, Initially, First of all

* second paragraph(negative): 
  However, On the other hand, In contrast, Nevertheless, Despite this

* conclusion: 
  In conclusion, To sum up, Overall, In summary, All in all

---

Try to build some complex sentences in your essay. You can use the following structures to create complex sentences:
1. Use subordinating conjunctions (e.g., although, because, since, while)
2. Use conditional clauses (e.g., if, unless, provided that)
3. Use comparative clauses (e.g., than, as...as, more...than)
4. Use correlative conjunctions (e.g., either...or, neither...nor, not only...but also)
