# Task 2 — Formal Letter Template
**50 min · ≥ 220 words · Formal register**

Use this for: complaint to MP / council, suggestion, request, enquiry.
For complaint about a service/product → use `2_complaint_letter.md` instead.

---

## Shell

```
[Your full name]
[Your address, line 1]
[Your address, line 2]
[Postcode]
[Your email — optional]

[Date — e.g. 17 July 2026]

[Recipient name, title]
[Organisation]
[Address line 1]
[Address line 2]
[Postcode]


Dear [Mr/Ms SURNAME] / Dear Sir or Madam,

[PARAGRAPH 1 — Purpose: why you are writing]
I am writing to [express my concern about / draw your attention to / request information regarding] [ISSUE/TOPIC]. As a [resident / student / member of the community], I feel strongly that [BRIEF STATEMENT OF YOUR POSITION].

[PARAGRAPH 2 — Problem / Situation: describe the issue with facts]
[PROBLEM 1]. [SUPPORTING DETAIL — what you observed, when, how often]. The situation is made worse by the fact that [ADDITIONAL FACTOR]. [IMPACT — what effect this has on people].

[PARAGRAPH 3 — Problem or Argument 2]
Furthermore, [SECOND POINT]. [EVIDENCE OR EXAMPLE]. This not only [CONSEQUENCE 1] but also [CONSEQUENCE 2]. If no action is taken, [PREDICTED NEGATIVE OUTCOME].

[PARAGRAPH 4 — Requested action]
I would therefore urge you to [SPECIFIC ACTION 1]. In addition, I would strongly recommend that [SPECIFIC ACTION 2]. I believe that these measures would [POSITIVE OUTCOME] and significantly [BENEFIT TO COMMUNITY].

I hope you will give this matter your urgent attention. I would be happy to discuss this further at your convenience.

Yours sincerely,  [← use when you know the person's name]
Yours faithfully, [← use when you wrote "Dear Sir or Madam"]

[Your full name]
```

---

## Key rules

| Rule | Example |
|------|---------|
| No contractions | do not / I am / it is (never don't / I'm / it's) |
| No emotional language | "I am concerned that..." not "I am furious about..." |
| Facts, not feelings | "The bins overflow twice a week" not "It is disgusting" |
| Yours sincerely | when you know the name (Dear Mr Smith) |
| Yours faithfully | when you don't know (Dear Sir or Madam) |
| One specific action per paragraph | don't list 5 actions in one paragraph |

---

## Word count guide

| Section | Target |
|---------|--------|
| Para 1 — Purpose | 2–3 sentences |
| Para 2 — Issue 1 | 3–4 sentences |
| Para 3 — Issue 2 / Argument | 3–4 sentences |
| Para 4 — Action request | 2–3 sentences |
| Closing line | 1 sentence |
| **Total** | **≥ 220 words** |

---

## Self-check

- [ ] Address and date at the top
- [ ] Correct greeting (Dear + name or Dear Sir/Madam)
- [ ] Para 1: states purpose clearly
- [ ] Para 2–3: specific facts, not vague feelings
- [ ] Para 4: at least one specific action requested
- [ ] Correct sign-off (sincerely / faithfully)
- [ ] Word count ≥ 220
- [ ] No contractions anywhere
