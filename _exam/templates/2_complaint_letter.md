# Template — Complaint Letter

> Memorise this shell. On exam day: fill in the [BRACKETS] only.
> Target: 150–180 words · Time: ≤ 20 min · Register: FORMAL throughout

---

Dear Sir or Madam,

I am writing to express my dissatisfaction with [PRODUCT / SERVICE: e.g. the catering service / the hotel room / the gym membership] that I [purchased / received / experienced] on [DATE or recent period].

Firstly, I was disappointed to find that [PROBLEM 1: state the fact, no emotion]. This was particularly unacceptable as [REASON / WHAT WAS PROMISED in advert or booking].

Furthermore, [PROBLEM 2: state the fact]. I had expected [WHAT SHOULD HAVE HAPPENED], yet [WHAT ACTUALLY HAPPENED].

[OPTIONAL — PROBLEM 3]: In addition, [PROBLEM 3: one sentence, fact only].

I would therefore [CHOOSE ONE]:
- request a full refund of [AMOUNT]
- ask you to replace the [ITEM] as soon as possible
- expect appropriate compensation for the inconvenience caused

I look forward to your prompt response and trust that this matter will be dealt with efficiently.

Yours faithfully,
[YOUR NAME]

---

> **Slots to fill: ~10 words of specific content. Everything else is fixed.**
> **Never use contractions (don't / I'm / it's) — always write in full.**
