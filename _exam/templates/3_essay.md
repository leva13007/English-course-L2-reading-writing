# Task 3 — Essay Template
**50 min · ≥ 200 words · Formal register**

Structure: **10 sentences minimum** — each ~20+ words.
Formula: 1 intro + (4 + 4) body + 1 conclusion.

---

## Shell

```
[INTRODUCTION — 1 sentence]
[TOPIC] is a subject that has attracted considerable debate in recent years.
While some argue that [POSITION A], others maintain that [POSITION B].
This essay will examine both perspectives before reaching a conclusion.

[BODY PARAGRAPH 1 — Positive argument — 4 sentences]
Firstly, [MAIN POSITIVE POINT — topic sentence].
For instance, [SPECIFIC EXAMPLE OR EVIDENCE that supports this point].
This is particularly significant because [WHY IT MATTERS / DEVELOPMENT].
As a result, [CONCLUSION / LINK — how this supports the overall argument].

[BODY PARAGRAPH 2 — Negative / contrasting argument — 4 sentences]
However, [CONTRASTING POINT — topic sentence].
For example, [SPECIFIC EXAMPLE OR EVIDENCE for the counter-argument].
Despite this, [NUANCE OR CONCESSION — acknowledge the other side].
Nevertheless, [CONCLUSION THAT STILL SUPPORTS YOUR POSITION].

[CONCLUSION — 1 sentence]
In conclusion, while [BRIEF SUMMARY OF BOTH SIDES],
it is clear that [YOUR FINAL POSITION / BALANCED JUDGEMENT].
Ultimately, [CLOSING THOUGHT — what should happen or what the future holds].
```

---

## Linking words by section

| Section | Openers |
|---------|---------|
| Introduction | It is widely acknowledged that · In recent years, · [TOPIC] has become a significant issue... |
| Body 1 (positive) | Firstly · To begin with · In the first place · One significant advantage is... |
| Body 2 (negative) | However · On the other hand · In contrast · Despite this · Nevertheless... |
| Conclusion | In conclusion · To sum up · Overall · In summary · All things considered... |

---

## Complex sentence structures to include

Use at least **3 of these** across the essay:

1. **Subordinating conjunction:** "Although social media connects people, it can also..."
2. **Conditional:** "If young people are not taught media literacy, they may..."
3. **Comparative:** "The benefits of X are far greater than the risks of Y."
4. **Correlative:** "Not only does X lead to Y, but it also..."
5. **Relative clause:** "This is a problem which affects millions of people worldwide."

## Time plan (50 min total)

| Step | Time |
|------|------|
| Read prompt + brainstorm ideas | 5 min |
| Write plan (thesis + 2 body points + conclusion phrase) | 5 min |
| Write the essay | 35 min |
| Proofread | 5 min |

## Self-check

- [ ] Introduction: topic introduced + position stated
- [ ] Body 1: topic sentence + example + development + mini-conclusion
- [ ] Body 2: contrasting point + example + nuance + conclusion
- [ ] Conclusion: both sides summarised + final position
- [ ] Word count ≥ 200
- [ ] At least 3 complex sentences
- [ ] Linking words used (not just "and" and "but")
- [ ] No contractions, no informal language
- [ ] Consistent formal register throughout
