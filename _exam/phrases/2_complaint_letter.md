# Phrase Bank — Complaint Letter

> ✅ = завжди безпечно · FORMAL only — no contractions, no casual language.

---

## Opening — FIXED (memorise word for word)
- ✅ `Dear Sir or Madam,`
- ✅ `I am writing to express my dissatisfaction with [PRODUCT/SERVICE] that I [purchased/received/experienced] on [DATE].`
- ✅ `I am writing to complain about [PRODUCT/SERVICE] which I [purchased/received] on [DATE].`

## Stating problems (use 2–3 of these)
- ✅ Firstly, I was disappointed to find that [FACT].
- ✅ To begin with, [FACT].
- ✅ Furthermore, [FACT]. I had expected [PROMISE], yet [REALITY].
- ✅ I must also point out that [FACT].
- ✅ In addition, [FACT], which I found entirely unacceptable.
- ✅ As if that were not enough, [FACT].

## Evidence / quoting the promise
- ✅ Your advertisement / brochure / website stated that [PROMISE].
- ✅ I had been assured that [PROMISE]; however, this was not the case.
- ✅ Contrary to what was advertised, [REALITY].

## Requesting action (choose 1 — be specific)
- ✅ I would therefore request a full refund of [AMOUNT].
- ✅ I would be grateful if you could replace the [ITEM] as soon as possible.
- ✅ I kindly ask that this matter be resolved within [X] working days.
- ✅ I believe I am entitled to [COMPENSATION], and I expect this to be arranged promptly.

## Closing — FIXED (memorise word for word)
- ✅ `I look forward to your prompt response.`
- ✅ `I trust that this matter will be dealt with efficiently.`
- ✅ `Yours faithfully,` ← always when opening is "Dear Sir or Madam"
- ✅ `Yours sincerely,` ← only if you used a name: "Dear Mr Smith"

---

> **Never:** contractions (I'm / don't / it's) · emotional language ("I am furious") · slang  
> **Always:** facts only in body · one clear request · formal closing formula
