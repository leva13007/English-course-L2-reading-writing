# Phrase Bank — Graph / Chart Report

> ✅ = завжди безпечно · ⚠️ = тільки якщо є зміна в часі

---

## Opening (choose 1 — memorise all)
- ✅ The graph shows [TOPIC] in [PLACE/PERIOD].
- ✅ The chart illustrates [TOPIC] between [YEAR] and [YEAR].
- ✅ The diagram presents data on [TOPIC] in [PLACE].

## Overall trend (1 sentence after intro)
- ✅ Overall, it is clear that [MAIN POINT].
- ✅ In general, the most notable feature is [EXTREME VALUE].
- ✅ At a glance, [CATEGORY] accounts for the largest proportion.

## Describing values
- ✅ [X] accounts for the largest share, at [N]%.
- ✅ [X] represents the smallest proportion, with only [N]%.
- ✅ Approximately [N]% of [GROUP] [VERB].
- ✅ [X] has the highest figure, at [N].

## Comparing two things
- ✅ While [X] stands at [N], [Y] is significantly higher / lower, at [N].
- ✅ [X] and [Y] are roughly equal, at [N] and [N] respectively.
- ✅ In contrast, [X] is considerably lower than [Y].

## Change over time ⚠️
- ⚠️ Between [YEAR] and [YEAR], [X] rose sharply / fell gradually / remained stable.
- ⚠️ There was a sharp increase / gradual decline in [X] from [N] to [N].
- ⚠️ [X] peaked at [N] in [YEAR] before falling to [N].

## Conclusion
- ✅ In conclusion, the data suggests that [MAIN FINDING in one sentence].
- ✅ Overall, it is evident that [MAIN FINDING].

---

> **Never write "I think" or "In my opinion" in a report — use impersonal structures only.**
