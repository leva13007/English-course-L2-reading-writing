# Phrase Bank — Review

> ✅ = завжди безпечно · All are semi-formal — opinions allowed here.

---

## Opening (choose 1)
- ✅ [TITLE] is a [GENRE] [film/book/album] by [NAME], released in [YEAR].
- ✅ [TITLE] is a [GENRE] [restaurant/venue] located in [PLACE], specialising in [TYPE].
- ✅ Directed by [NAME], [TITLE] is a [YEAR] [GENRE] film that [ONE-LINE PREMISE].

## Strengths / Positives
- ✅ One of the main strengths of [TITLE] is [SPECIFIC THING].
- ✅ Particularly impressive is [SPECIFIC THING].
- ✅ [TITLE] does an excellent job of [VERB+ing].
- ✅ The [acting / writing / atmosphere / cuisine] is outstanding / convincing / well-crafted.
- ✅ What makes [TITLE] stand out is [REASON].

## Weaknesses / Negatives (always soft — never harsh)
- ✅ However, one drawback is that [HONEST NEGATIVE].
- ✅ On the negative side, [THING] could perhaps be improved.
- ✅ The only weakness is [MINOR ISSUE].
- ✅ [TITLE] may not appeal to everyone, particularly those who [PREFERENCE].

## Recommendation / Conclusion (choose 1)
- ✅ Overall, I would highly recommend [TITLE] to [AUDIENCE: anyone who enjoys…].
- ✅ Despite its minor flaws, [TITLE] is a thoroughly enjoyable [film/read/experience].
- ✅ [TITLE] is well worth [watching/reading/visiting], especially if you enjoy [GENRE/TYPE].
- ✅ In conclusion, [TITLE] is an impressive [work/production/venue] that [MAIN QUALITY].

---

> **Balance = ~2 positives for every 1 negative.**  
> **Avoid: "amazing", "horrible", "I loved it" — too informal for C1.**  
> **Use instead: "outstanding", "disappointing", "thoroughly enjoyable".**
