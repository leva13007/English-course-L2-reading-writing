# Task 1 — Form Phrases
Phrase bank for Section 3 written responses. All formal register. ✅ = safe to use in any context.

---

## Motivation / Interest questions
*("Why are you interested?" / "What do you hope to gain?")*

| Phrase | Use |
|--------|-----|
| ✅ I am particularly interested in this opportunity because... | Opening — state your main reason |
| ✅ I believe this would provide me with an excellent opportunity to... | State the benefit you expect |
| ✅ Having read about [PROGRAMME/ROLE], I am convinced that... | Show you researched it |
| ✅ I am keen to develop my skills in [AREA], as I consider this essential for... | Link interest to your goals |
| ✅ I am confident that this experience will enable me to... | Forward-looking conclusion |
| ⚠️ This opportunity appeals to me greatly because... | Slightly more informal — use carefully |

---

## Skills / Qualities questions
*("What skills do you have?" / "What qualities would make you suitable?")*

| Phrase | Use |
|--------|-----|
| ✅ One of my key strengths is [SKILL], which enables me to... | Lead with your best skill |
| ✅ I have experience of [ACTIVITY], where I demonstrated [QUALITY] | Give a concrete example |
| ✅ I consider myself to be [adj + adj], and I believe these qualities would be an asset... | Describe yourself positively |
| ✅ Although I am still developing my [AREA], I am committed to improving through... | Honest but positive — shows self-awareness |
| ✅ I work well both independently and as part of a team, and I am able to... | Versatility phrase |
| ✅ In previous [roles / studies / experience], I have consistently [POSITIVE OUTCOME] | Pattern of success |

---

## Goals / Future plans questions
*("What are your future career goals?" / "Where do you see yourself in five years?")*

| Phrase | Use |
|--------|-----|
| ✅ In the long term, I aspire to [GOAL]... | Strong aspirational opener |
| ✅ My immediate aim is to [SHORT-TERM GOAL], after which I intend to... | Shows clear progression |
| ✅ Ultimately, I hope to [LONG-TERM VISION]... | Closing statement |
| ✅ I am determined to work hard to achieve [GOAL], and I believe that... | Shows commitment |
| ✅ I see this as an important step towards [CAREER PATH / QUALIFICATION]... | Connects opportunity to goals |
| ⚠️ One day, I would like to... | Too informal — replace with "In the long term, I aspire to..." |

---

## Describing experience
*("Do you have any relevant experience?" / "Describe your background.")*

| Phrase | Use |
|--------|-----|
| ✅ I have [X years of] experience in [FIELD], during which I... | State experience + what you did |
| ✅ In my previous role as [ROLE], I was responsible for... | Formal job description opener |
| ✅ I have volunteered with [ORGANISATION], where I developed skills in... | For volunteer / community work |
| ✅ Although I have not yet worked in [FIELD], I have [TRANSFERABLE EXPERIENCE]... | If experience is indirect |
| ✅ This experience taught me [LESSON / SKILL], which I believe is highly relevant to... | Reflect on what you learned |

---

## Complex sentence connectors (use in Section 3)

- **Reason:** because, as, since, given that
- **Contrast:** although, even though, despite the fact that, whereas
- **Addition:** furthermore, in addition, moreover, not only... but also
- **Result:** as a result, consequently, therefore, which means that
- **Condition:** if, provided that, as long as, unless
- **Relative:** which, where, who, whose, that

Aim: at least **5 complex sentences** across all Section 3 answers.
