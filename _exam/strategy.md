## Exam-day checklist

- [ ] Re-read the task twice before writing
- [ ] Mentally load the correct template
- [ ] Write intro → body → conclusion, no skipping
- [ ] Check: is the register formal throughout?
- [ ] Count words (rough estimate): too short = lose marks
- [ ] 2 min at the end: scan for contractions, spelling, punctuation
