# Exam Strategy — ESOL L2

**Date:** 17 July 2026 · Cardiff and Vale College
**Core idea:** freeze the shell, swap the content — memorise templates + phrases, apply to any topic.

---

## Writing paper — task types

| # | Task | Time limit | Word count |
|---|------|-----------|------------|
| 1 | **Form** — fill in personal details + write responses in full sentences | 30 min | ≥ 200 |
| 2 | **Formal writing** — letter / complaint / review / personal statement | 50 min | ≥ 220 |
| 3 | **Extended writing** — essay / article / advice | 50 min | ≥ 200 |

---

## Task 1 — Form

Three sections:
- **Section 1:** Personal details (name, DOB, address, phone, email) — short factual answers
- **Section 2:** Multiple choice — tick / circle / delete as instructed (no writing)
- **Section 3:** Written responses — answer 3–4 questions in **full sentences**, 3–5 sentences each

Key rule: Section 3 must have **at least 5 correct sentences total** across all questions. Section 1 capitals/lower case must be exact; address order: building number → street name → town/city → postcode.

**Marking insight:** Examiners select only the **5 best sentences** from across the whole of Section 3 and mark only those — every other sentence is ignored entirely, errors included, they are not penalised. → Aim to write **7–8 sentences**, not just 5, so the weakest ones can be "sacrificed" and you can risk 1–2 more ambitious, complex sentences without fear of losing marks.

→ Template: `templates/1_form.md` · Phrases: `phrases/1_form.md`

## Task 2 — Formal writing

Choose the right sub-template depending on what the task asks:

| Sub-type | When | Template |
|----------|------|---------|
| Formal letter (general) | Complaint to MP, request, suggestion | `templates/2_formal_letter.md` |
| Complaint letter | Unhappy with service / product | `templates/2_complaint_letter.md` |
| Review | Film / book / restaurant / music | `templates/2_review.md` |

Register: **formal throughout** — no contractions, no slang.

## Task 3 — Extended writing

| Sub-type | Structure |
|----------|-----------|
| Essay | Intro (1) + Body ×2 (4 sentences each) + Conclusion (1) = 10 sentences min |
| Article | Heading + engaging intro + 2–3 paragraphs + conclusion |
| Advice | Opening + 3–4 advice points with reasons + closing |

→ Template: `templates/3_essay.md`

## Exam-day checklist

- [ ] Re-read the task twice before writing
- [ ] Identify which task type / sub-type it is
- [ ] Mentally load the correct template
- [ ] Section 3 (Task 1): write 7–8 full sentences, not just 5 — only your best 5 are marked, the rest are ignored, so include complex structures without fear
- [ ] Tasks 2 & 3: write intro → body → conclusion, no skipping
- [ ] Check register: formal throughout, no contractions
- [ ] Count words (rough estimate): too short = lose marks
- [ ] 2 min at the end: scan for grammar, spelling, punctuation
