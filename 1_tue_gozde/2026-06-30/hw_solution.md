## Version 1 — My First Attempt


Task 2 Correspondence (Guide time 50 minutes)

Your employer has asked members of staff for their opinions on how the room used for staff breaks could be improved. Write a letter to Head Office giving your views on why the room is unsuitable at the moment and proposing some changes. Write to: Head Office, Home Retailers, 28 Lord Road, Southway, S1 6PK You must plan the points you are going to include and how you are going to introduce, develop and finish your writing. Write using paragraphs. Write 200 or more words.

Plan your letter here:

Address:
Purpose: To improve the staff break room facilities
Issue1: Not enough space (a lot of unnecessary furniture), uncomfortable chairs
Issue2: poor lighting, no heating.
Proposed changes: Remove unnecessary furniture, add more comfortable chairs, improve lighting, add heating.
Closing statement: wait for a response, happy to discuss further.

Letter here:

Head Office,
Home Retailers,
28 Lord Road,
Southway, S1 6PK

Dear Sir or Madam,
I am writing to express my concern about the current state of the staff break room and to propose some improvements. As a member of the staff, I feel strongly that the room is unsuitable for its intended purpose and requires immediate attention.
There are several issues with the current setup of the room. It is currently overcrowded with unnecessary furniture, which limits the available space for staff to relax during their breaks. Additionally, the chairs provided are uncomfortable, making it difficult for employees to unwind and recharge. This lack of comfort negatively impacts staff morale and productivity.
Furthermore, the lighting in the room is inadequate, creating a dim and uninviting atmosphere. The absence of proper heating during colder months also makes the room uncomfortable to use. These factors contribute to a less than ideal environment for staff to take their breaks.
We as staff would greatly appreciate it if the following changes could be implemented: removing unnecessary furniture to create more space, providing more comfortable chairs, improving the lighting, and installing a heating system. These improvements would significantly enhance the overall experience of using the break room and contribute to a more positive work environment.
Thank you for considering my suggestions. I look forward to your response and would be happy to discuss these ideas further if needed.
Yours faithfully,
[Your Name]
